Plant and LEAP port setup

Fixed dual-port topology (MOD54417)

This gateway uses independent Ethernet ports. Bridging both ports is not supported.

  • Port 1 (Plant Network) - PLC / EtherNet/IP and this web UI. Connect to your plant subnet.
  • Port 2 (LEAP Network) - LEAP devices only. Keep LEAP peers on a separate subnet or direct cable.
